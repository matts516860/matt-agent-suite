# Trip Planning Workflow (Redwood → Crater Lake → Portland Example)

This workflow covers multi-day road trip planning, optimized for Matt and his 11-year-old son, Ben.

## 1. Input

- Date range (e.g., Aug 3–9).
- Lodging info (if already booked).
- Starting and ending locations.
- Driving preferences (e.g., low driving in afternoons).

## 2. Trip Concierge Agent

- Designs base itinerary:
  - Each day broken into Morning / Afternoon / Evening.
  - Drive times staggered to avoid fatigue.
  - Key stops: redwoods, viewpoints, Crater Lake rim, waterfalls, coastal towns.
  - Includes optional extras (short hikes, beaches, visitor centers).
- Marks:
  - Must-see highlights.
  - Nice-to-have options.
  - Rain or smoke alternatives.

**Output:**  
Day-by-day trip plan with distances, estimated time, and difficulty.

## 3. Home & Life Assistant Agent

- Generates:
  - Packing lists (clothes, gear, snacks, car items).
  - Car readiness checklist (basic safety, not mechanical teardown).
  - Entertainment plan for Ben (games, music, downloads).

## 4. Trip Concierge Agent (Refinement)

- Creates a **“Ben-first”** version:
  - More emphasis on fun, shorter hikes, and cool stops.
  - Clear expectations for when driving vs exploring happens.

**Final Outcome:**  
A ready-to-execute trip plan that is realistic, kid-friendly, and resilient to weather changes.
